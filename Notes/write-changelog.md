# Write Changelog

Newest on top. Each entry is something you can try yourself.

## v0.95

- MCP for outside agents, done right: connecting Write to Claude, Cursor, or
  Codex is now a copy-paste step, and those agents get a clean set of tools
  plus your tags and note links. The guide lives at write.ramine.net/docs/ai.
- Workspace polish: press Cmd and a number to jump to a folder, or a plain
  number to open that item in the list; the sidebar and the list no longer
  fight over what is selected; Home search sits on the toolbar; Recent has a
  list, column, or grid toggle; the star moved to the left of each item; and
  drag-selecting no longer highlights text.

## v0.94

- Tags: add tags to any note, post, or bookmark and see everything with that
  tag across folders. Type # in the tag field to pick or create one. Your blog
  gets public tag pages; notes and bookmarks stay private.
- Note links: type [[ to link to another note (or create it), and every note
  shows what links to it. Links to private notes stay private on your blog.
- Quick capture: press the global hotkey or pick New note in the menu bar to
  jot a note from any app. It saves even if you are offline.

## v0.88 to v0.93, the short version

- A Superhuman-style redesign across the workspace: search opens as a full
  page, a star marks favorites, pin keeps any item at the top, multi-select
  with a bottom action bar, and a cleaner editor. The second line of a blog
  post is now its subtitle.
- Builds ship themselves: every new change is built and released automatically,
  everywhere, with no manual step.
- Fixes: a note that got stuck "Syncing 1 file", and the assistant's "Local
  model unavailable" error.

## v0.87

- Shortcuts that just work: the Write actions in the Shortcuts app (Create
  document, Search documents, Append text, Move, Publish, Bookmark a URL) now
  talk straight to your workspace, so they work anywhere, even before Finder
  has downloaded a single file.

## v0.86

- One Write, one truth: the old iCloud Drive/Write folder is retired. The
  Write location in Finder's sidebar IS your workspace now, and the menu bar
  status reflects it live. Spotlight finds your posts again too.

## v0.83 to v0.85

- Your posts in Finder are now .textpack files, the zipped TextBundle format
  that Bear, Ulysses, and iA Writer read. Double-click one and it opens in
  Write; drag one into Bear and it imports with its images.
- Renaming a post in Finder now always sticks. The rename bug that snapped
  names back is structurally gone.

## v0.77 to v0.81

- Co-writing you can trust: closing a tab mid-edit can no longer lose words,
  and a stale editing session can never overwrite newer work. Try slamming a
  tab shut right after typing; your words arrive anyway.
- Optional cloud AI (off by default): with a gateway key set, the assistant
  can hand long jobs to a hosted model. On-device AI stays the default.

## v0.30 to v0.76, the short version

- Sync became bulletproof: every save carries a revision check, so two writers
  can never silently clobber each other.
- Write in Finder grew up: files named by title, one Write location with your
  workspace inside, renames and edits sync both ways within seconds, and
  everything stays downloaded (no hollow cloud icons).
- The app got fast: command bar (Cmd-K), keyboard shortcuts everywhere,
  instant folder switching.
- The assistant arrived: on-device Apple intelligence for titles, tags,
  summaries, rewrites, and agent commands, plus connectors for outside AIs.
- Sharing: send pages and text to Write from any app, Quick Look previews,
  and your posts in Spotlight.

## v0.29

- Write in Finder: Write now shows up in the Finder sidebar under Locations.
  Open it to see Blog, Notes, and Bookmarks; open a folder to see your posts,
  notes, and bookmarks as files that download the moment you open them. Edit a
  file and save, and the change syncs to your workspace. You can also create,
  rename, move, and delete files right in Finder. Your existing iCloud
  Drive/Write folder stays exactly as it was for now (two views during the
  transition); the old folder gets retired later, once this feels right.

## Live updates (Jul 12)

- The workspace now stays up to date on its own. When something changes your
  content outside the current view (a shared item, a Finder edit, another
  device), the note, post, or bookmark appears in the list without a manual
  reload. Try it: with the app open on Notes, share a page from Safari, or add
  a file in Finder, and watch it show up.

## v0.28

- Share to Write: in Safari or any app, click Share and pick Write. A web page
  is saved as a bookmark, selected text as a note, straight into your workspace.
  It shows up in Notes or Bookmarks within a second or two.
- Quick Look preview: press Space on any `.md` file inside iCloud Drive/Write in
  Finder to see a clean rendered preview (headings, lists, quotes, code). The
  private metadata lines are hidden and the title becomes the heading.

## v0.26

- Shortcuts support: open the Shortcuts app, add an action, and search "Write".
  Ten actions are available (create, open, append to, search, move, and publish
  a document, create a folder, create a bookmark from a URL, and get recent
  documents).

## v0.24

- Fixed: the app no longer shows a blank white window after the screen wakes,
  the display sleeps, or you switch away and back.

## v0.23

- Native editor: open a note or post file to edit it in a native macOS window
  with Apple Writing Tools on the body (proofread, rewrite, summarize, change
  tone). Code blocks are left untouched.
- Spotlight search: your Write notes, posts, and bookmarks are searchable in
  macOS Spotlight. Click a result to jump straight to that item in the app.

## Workspace (web)

- Empty Trash: the Trash view has an Empty Trash button that permanently clears
  every soft-deleted item after a confirmation.
- Your whole workspace lives as plain Markdown files in iCloud Drive/Write, so
  you can open, edit, rename, and move notes, posts, and bookmarks in Finder and
  the app keeps up.
