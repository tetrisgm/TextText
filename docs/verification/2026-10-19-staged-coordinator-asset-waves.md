## Contract

Opt-in covers unchanged-path/asset plain append and compatible mixed schedules, at most 100 rounds. Replacement, move, restart, offline/fault, close and soak combinations are rejected before prepare. Order: checked hello identity/capability/session, existing read-only full-snapshot baseline observe, content accounting and output forecast, three complete fresh-UUID proof waves, then round prepare/edit. Each baseline, postround and independent reopen phase has a fresh bounded oracle.

Actual returned proofs, full snapshots, four validated-live/two raw markers and role-qualified identity/session/generation pins are checked. Expected asset hashes are comparison pins only. Full-document accounting preserves hidden fields and absent subtitle. Wrong/missing/stale/duplicate evidence stops first failure; unavailable producers are not normalized into successful/retryable evidence. A round is not counted complete before its proof passes.

The output forecast uses actual baseline/planned growth plus protocol reserve and the required three waves, not all possible retries. Existing 256KiB message/64MiB per-role limits, actual byte ledger, phase deadline and maximum 256 waves bound retries. Arbitrary-size documents/churn need not pass. Real zero-round reopen additionally requires the existing matching physical mutation receipt, exact prior phase/path/assets/full hash and internally consistent trailing wave hashes/stability counts. This is consistency checking, not cryptographic receipt authentication.
