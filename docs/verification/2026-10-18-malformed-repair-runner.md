actor journey, Oracle deployment, Windows filesystem campaign, or six-client
physical acceptance was performed for this slice.

or a ZIP whose canonical document.json is incomplete JSON. Both the original
and fault bytes are retained. Install/presence/repair are separate JSONL phases
so the actor's serialized dispatcher remains responsive. Every fault proof is

actual providers without reopening them, verifies their previous full semantic

hold duration. Guarded repair precedes six normal edits. Final all-six full
semantic accounting and three convergence samples remain mandatory. Reopen
binds the full hash and exact fault config to the prior passing real receipt.

fault evidence fails. Cleanup cannot convert failure into acceptance.
